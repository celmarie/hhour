Appie Hour

Delete your account & data

You can delete your Appie Hour account and its associated data at any time. Deletion is permanent.

Delete directly in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Appie Hour and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Open Privacy & Data
  4. Choose Delete my account and confirm

Or request deletion without the app

Visit appiehour.com/support and send us a message from the email address linked to your account asking for account deletion. We verify the request via a code sent to that email.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, email, birthdate), saved deals, vouchers and purchase history, submitted deals and photos, notification subscriptions, and credits. Records we must keep for legal/accounting reasons (e.g. payment records) are retained only as required by law, then removed.
